Audience Bar

Successfully perform actions to increase the large bottom bar. The rate it increases by will be calculated from the sum of your party's Appeal, but for testing purposes you can change it at will. Max it out at least one time to win a contest and be awarded with a ribbon and contest tokens.

The audience will call for an encore when you max out the bar. This slightly increases the audience bar. Encores act as point multipliers.

Gameplay gimmicks

The bars underneath the individual pokemon change depending on their status. Successfully performing an action makes the pokemon appeal to the audience (💖). If the action results in a failure, the pokemon is jammed (🖤). There are three gameplay styles. Click on the "Rank Gameplay" tab for more information on this Hall's game style.

Crowd Hype and Frenzy Mode

When all pokemon on stage have performed, their statuses are evaluated to generate Crowd Hype. A fully hyped crowd will induce Frenzy Mode. Hype will not go up if a Pokemon is jammed.

In Hoenn, jamming prevents Crowd Hype from being generated and reduces the amount of audience Appeal that is produced for a short while.

In Sinnoh, jammed Pokemon decrease the hype bar by 1 with no extra effects.

Gameplay is slightly altered during Frenzy Mode and chains cannot be broken for its duration. Your audience appeal dealt will also be buffed based on how many contests you've won.

Extra points and rewards can be rewarded during Frenzy Mode.

Scoring

Successful actions increase the chain, add to your score, and can give a small bonus dealt audience appeal. Chains are broken when a poor action is performed.

The higher your score the more tokens you get upon completion.
